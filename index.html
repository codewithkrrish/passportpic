Size

Position

Positioning is clamped so the crop frame always stays fully covered by the photo.

Background

The cutout assist keys out colours near the photo's own background (sampled from its corners). It works best on plain, even backgrounds; it will soften hair edges and struggle with patterned backgrounds. When it is off, the chosen colour only fills the area outside the photo, if any.

Export

The single photo exports at the exact preset size at 300 DPI. The sheet is a 1200 x 1800 px PNG (4 x 6 inch at 300 DPI) with your chosen number of copies, ready to print.
